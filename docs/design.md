## 1. Shop Page Components (`shop.html`)
- **Product Card**:
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, Radius `20px`, Hover Elevation
  - Product Image with Aspect Ratio 4:3
  - Key Specs Bullet Points with check icons
  - Price Tag with bold typography `#1E3A8A`
- **Pillow Finder Quiz Box**:
  - Multi-step interactive card container with step progress indicator (Step 1 of 3)
  - Choice Buttons with icon, title, and descriptive subtitle
  - Animated results card with circular or radial match gauge and customized recommendation badge
- **Filter Tags**:
  - Horizontal pill buttons with active state `#2563EB` and light hover state `#EFF6FF`
